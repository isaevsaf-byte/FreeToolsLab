One free tool a week for people who buy things.
No sign-up. Nothing leaves your browser. This is number one.

£5,918 a year leaves the deal when 30-day terms become 60.

Not saved by you. Not earned by the supplier.
Its bank has it.

Longer terms are a loan. 30 extra days on £1.2m is £98,630.
Your money costs, say, 6%. That is £5,918 a year.
The supplier funds it at its rate, say 12%: £11,836 a year.
Half of that leaves the pair. Neither of you books it.

For a £4m supplier that is 3.7% of its profit.

One principle: never price a terms change at one rate.
The loan costs what the lender pays, not what the borrower saves.

The tool shows both sides on one screen.
Your number, the supplier's, and a discount that beats the extension.
Switch to "I am the supplier" and send it to yours.

What rate does your smallest strategic supplier borrow at?

Live: freetoolslab.org/tools/payment-terms-lens/ · Tell me what broke.
